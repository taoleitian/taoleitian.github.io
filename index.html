Leitian Tao

I am a Ph.D. student in the Computer Sciences Department at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. My current research interests focus on post-training of large language models, AI agents, and multi-modality.

I work as a research assistant at UW–Madison, under the supervision of Sharon Li.

I am currently working as a Research Scientist Intern at Meta FAIR with Dr. Jason Weston and Dr. Ping Yu.

Email  /  Google Scholar  /  Github  /  LinkedIn  /  CV

Research

My research focuses on LLM alignment and post-training, reasoning with synthetic data, and open-world robustness. Recent work shows that synthetic data from smaller, weaker models can be more effective than data from larger models for improving LLM reasoning. Representative papers are highlighted.
